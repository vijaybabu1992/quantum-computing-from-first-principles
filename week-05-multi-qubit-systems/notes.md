💡 MULTI-QUBIT SYSTEMS (IN SIMPLE TERMS)

When we move from one qubit to two qubits, we don’t just add states.
We multiply the state space.

This is done using the tensor product.

• 1 qubit → 2 states
• 2 qubits → 4 states
• n qubits → 2ⁿ states

The math grows exponentially — even though the rules stay the same.

📘 WHAT I LEARNED THIS WEEK

• Multi-qubit states are built using tensor products
• |00⟩, |01⟩, |10⟩, |11⟩ form the basis for two qubits
• A joint state is a single vector, not two separate ones
• Tensor products explain why quantum systems scale so fast

🧠 KEY INSIGHT FROM WEEK 5

The power of quantum computing is not in single qubits.

It comes from how state spaces grow exponentially
while operations remain linear.

Once I saw tensor products as structured vector construction,
multi-qubit systems felt logical instead of overwhelming.
